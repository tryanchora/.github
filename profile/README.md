# Anchora

**Your study-abroad journey, under control.**

Anchora is a study-abroad planner for independent students in India applying to universities in Germany without a consultancy. It turns the patchwork of university websites, official portals, spreadsheets and chat threads into one plan: clear stages, honest dates, your documents, and one obvious next step.

- **Your journey:** stages and tasks set up for your intake and degree, with what's next and what's blocking you.
- **Your documents:** private storage with version history.
- **Atlas, the AI guide:** explains your plan, cites official sources, and proposes changes that you accept or decline. Atlas stays off until you turn it on.

Anchora is in a pilot and open by invitation from the waitlist.
